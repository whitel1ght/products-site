Header Tool privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-09-29

Header Tool does not collect, sell or share your browsing data, and contains no analytics, telemetry or ads.

Data stored on your device

Stored in chrome.storage.local (not synced by the extension):

  • Your profiles and rules: names, URL filters, header names and values, redirect targets and resource types.
  • The pause switch and the active profile.
  • A cached flag saying whether Pro is unlocked.

The payment library used for Pro (ExtensionPay) also stores an anonymous key and cached account status in Chrome extension storage (chrome.storage.sync where available, so Chrome may sync it across your signed-in browsers).

Header values you enter are applied to requests by Chrome itself using declarativeNetRequest. The extension does not read page content, requests, responses, cookies or browsing history.

Network requests

The only network requests the extension makes are to ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) to check Pro status. They occur when the popup or options page opens, when the browser starts, and every few seconds for up to two minutes after you click Upgrade. ExtensionPay receives an anonymous install key generated by its library and, if you pay or log in, your email address and payment identity. Payments are processed by Stripe through ExtensionPay; the extension never sees card details. No rules, headers, URLs or browsing data are sent. See ExtensionPay's privacy policy.

Permissions

The extension requests storage and declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess. Site access is an optional permission requested when you add rules and is used only to apply your own rules.
